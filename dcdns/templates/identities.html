Prototype key custody. For demonstration convenience this portal holds the private keys on disk and signs on behalf of the selected identity. In a real deployment signing happens on the client — a browser wallet, smart card or department HSM — and the private key never reaches the server. This is the single largest simplification in the prototype and is documented as such.

What each role may do

RolePermissions
root Create and revoke top-level campus namespaces; delegate the root zone.
department Manage its namespace, delegate sub-namespaces, grant registrar rights, register names, revoke names inside its subtree.
student Register names where granted, update records of names it owns, transfer ownership, revoke its own names.
outsider May submit signed operations; every one is rejected because it holds no delegated authority.
